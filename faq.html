Rooftop solar ke baare mein aapke sawalo ke jawab

Cost se lekar maintenance tak — sabse zyada puche jaane wale sawal, saaf aur simple bhasha mein.

System size par depend karta hai — ghar ke liye typically 1kW se 5kW tak ka system common hai. Approx cost ₹55,000–₹65,000 per kW hoti hai, lekin PM Surya Ghar subsidy (₹30,000 se ₹78,000 tak) minus hone ke baad aapki actual investment kaafi kam ho jati hai. Exact amount jaanne ke liye humara Subsidy Calculator use kare ya free site survey book kare.
Rajasthan jaisi excellent-sun region mein 3 kW system roughly 12–13 units/day generate karta hai, matlab lagbhag 360–400 units/month. Ye output weather aur season ke hisaab se thoda upar-neeche ho sakta hai.
Agar aapka system aapki monthly consumption ke hisaab se sahi size ka hai, toh bill almost zero ya sirf fixed/minimum charges tak reduce ho jata hai. Net metering ki wajah se agar aap zyada bijli banate hai toh extra units grid ko export hoke agle bill mein credit hoti hai.
Subsidy PM Surya Ghar national portal par registration, DISCOM approval aur installation verification ke baad seedha aapke bank account mein aati hai. Ye poora paperwork process — registration se lekar final approval tak — Boston Services Solar khud handle karti hai, aapko sirf documents provide karne hote hai.
Net metering ek special bi-directional meter hai jo dono track karta hai — aap grid se kitni bijli le rahe hai aur apni solar se kitni extra bijli grid ko de rahe hai. Extra units automatically aapke bill mein credit ho jati hai, jisse overall bill aur kam ho jata hai.
Quality panels ki performance warranty typically 25 years tak hoti hai. Panels ka output slowly degrade hota hai (roughly 0.5% per year), matlab 25 saal baad bhi wo apni original capacity ka lagbhag 80-85% generate karte rehte hai.
Rooftop solar mein maintenance minimal hoti hai — mainly periodic panel cleaning (dust/dirt hatane ke liye) aur saal mein ek-do baar general health check. Boston Services affordable AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) plans bhi offer karti hai jisse aapko kuch alag se sochna nahi padta.
Haan, panels cloudy ya rainy din mein bhi bijli banate hai — bas output thoda kam (roughly 10-25%) ho jata hai diffused sunlight ki wajah se. Kyunki aapka system grid-connected hota hai, kam generation wale din grid se seedha bijli mil jati hai, koi disruption nahi hota.
Roughly 90–100 sq. ft. shadow-free space per kW chahiye hoti hai. Matlab 3kW system ke liye approx 270–300 sq. ft. ki flat, clean rooftop area sufficient hoti hai. Hamari team free site survey ke dauran exact layout batati hai.
Actual physical installation sirf 2-3 din mein complete ho jata hai. Uske baad net metering aur subsidy approval ke liye DISCOM process mein typically 2-4 hafte lagte hai — is poore process mein Boston Services Solar aapke saath rehti hai.
